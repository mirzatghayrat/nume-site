A little help.

For questions about NUME, contact mirzatghayrat@gmail.com. Support is provided by Mierzati Aireti.

How do I play?

Choose a chapter and a level. Drag a number or symbol from the inventory onto the board. When you build a valid equation, tap the equals sign to evaluate it. The result changes the route NUME can take to the door. Use Undo or Reset to try again.

What is free?

The first two chapters contain 16 free levels. One non-consumable App Store purchase unlocks Chapters 3–6, with 32 additional levels. There are no ads, subscriptions, energy meters or paid hints. The current price is shown by the App Store in your region.

How do I restore the full game?

Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Connect to the internet, open NUME’s Settings, and choose Restore Purchases. You can also restore from the full-game purchase screen. Restoring a purchase does not restore completed levels or best scores.

The store is unavailable

Check your internet connection and App Store sign-in, then choose Retry. Availability also depends on your App Store country or region. If it continues, send us your device model, iOS version and the message you see. Do not send payment details or your Apple Account password.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress and settings are stored on your device. NUME does not provide game-account or iCloud progress sync. Deleting the app can remove local progress. An Apple device backup may include it, depending on your backup settings.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The puzzles work offline. Buying the full game and restoring purchases require an internet connection.

Sound, haptics and motion

Open Settings to adjust sound volume, switch haptics, or choose notebook, blackboard or system appearance. Reduced motion follows your device’s accessibility setting.

Refunds

Apple processes App Store payments and refund requests. Visit Apple’s Report a Problem page for available options. We cannot access your card or approve an Apple refund directly.

Report a problem

Include the chapter and level, your device model, iOS version, and app version shown in Settings. A screenshot and a short description are helpful. Remove personal information before sending an image.
