Privacy Policy

NUME is a single-player puzzle game provided by Mierzati Aireti. This policy covers the NUME app and this support website.

Your game stays on your device

NUME does not operate a game account, advertising network or developer analytics service. We do not collect or transmit your gameplay data to our servers. The app includes no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Saved progress and preferences

Your completed levels, best step counts, tutorial progress, appearance, sound and haptic preferences are stored locally on your device. They may be included in device backups managed by Apple. NUME does not provide game-account or iCloud progress sync. Deleting the app removes its local data; restoring a device backup may restore that data.

App Store purchases

Apple processes payments and manages purchase records. NUME uses Apple’s StoreKit to check whether you own the full game and to restore that purchase. We do not receive your card details. Purchases and restoration require a network connection and are subject to Apple’s privacy policy.

Permissions and tracking

NUME does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or advertising identifier. It does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send and use them to respond. Our support mailbox is hosted by Google. Avoid sending passwords, card details, identity documents or unnecessary personal information. Correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal requirements. You can request deletion by contacting the address below.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics cookies, advertising scripts or tracking pixels. Images, fonts and video are served from this site. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Email and external links open services with their own privacy practices.

Children

NUME has no in-game chat, social profile, targeted advertising or developer data collection. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If a child has sent personal information to our support mailbox, a parent or guardian can request its deletion.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated. The effective date above identifies this version.
